How to Play

  • Open a square: click / tap it. Numbers show how many mines touch it.
  • Flag a mine: right-click, or long-press on a phone.
  • Chord: middle-click, or press both mouse buttons, on a number whose flags match it. On a phone, tap a number to chord.
  • Win: open every square that is not a mine.
  • Lucky tokens 🎁: every 10 safe squares you earn a surprise token, at random: 🔍 Scanner (tap a square to see the mines in its 3×3 area), 🛡️ Shield (blocks the next mine you hit), or ✨ Lucky Reveal (opens a random safe square). Tap a token to use it.
  • Shortcuts: F2 new game, Esc cancels a token.
