How to Play

Goal: open every tile that doesn't hide a mine.

  • Click / tap a tile to open it. Numbers count the mines next to it.
  • Right-click (or long-press on touch) to flag a tile. With Question marks on, flagging again adds a “?”.
  • Click a number whose flags match it (or press both mouse buttons, or middle-click) to open its neighbours.
  • F2 or the 🙂 face starts a new game.

✨ Lucky Tiles: about 1 in 70 safe tiles hides a surprise. They look like any other tile until you open one. You get a random power-up:

  • 🛡️ Shield — the next mine you hit is defused instead of exploding.
  • 📡 Sonar — click to open a random safe tile.
  • 🧲 Magnet — click to flag a random mine.
